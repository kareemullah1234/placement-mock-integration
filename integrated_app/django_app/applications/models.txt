App: applications

Model: Application
- candidate: ForeignKey(CandidateProfile, on_delete=CASCADE)
- job: ForeignKey(JobPost, on_delete=CASCADE)
- status: CharField(max_length=20, choices=STATUS_CHOICES, default='applied')
- applied_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)
- updated_at: DateTimeField(auto_now=True)

Status Choices:
- applied
- shortlisted
- rejected
- aptitude_passed
- aptitude_failed
- mock_passed
- mock_failed
- final_selected
