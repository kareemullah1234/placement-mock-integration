Complete Interview Guide
Scheduling Rules:
  • • NO next-day slots allowed - Minimum 48 hours required
  • • Provide 3 different time slots for scheduling flexibility
  • • Once approved, you have limited time to start the interview
Interview Process (Step by Step):
  1. 1. Pre-Interview: Grant camera/microphone permissions (one-time only)
  2. 2. Enter Fullscreen: Mandatory for proctoring - cannot exit during interview
  3. 3. Question Flow: Click "Play Question" → Listen carefully → Can replay if needed
  4. 4. Recording: Click "Start Recording" → Speak your answer clearly → Click "Stop Recording"
  5. 5. Next Question: Click "Next Question" to proceed (total 6 questions)
  6. 6. End Interview: Click "End Interview" → Wait 2 minutes for processing → Auto-redirect
Proctoring rules
  • • Fullscreen mandatory - Exiting fullscreen = violation
  • • No tab switching - Switching tabs = violation
  • • No window minimizing - Minimizing = violation
  • • Maximum 2 violations allowed - 3rd violation = auto-termination
  • • Stay focused - Look at camera while speaking
  • • No external help - No notes, no other people, no additional devices
Environment Setup:
  • • Quiet room: No background noise or interruptions
  • • Good lighting: Face should be clearly visible
  • • Desktop/Laptop preferred: Mobile devices not recommended
  • • Browser: Use Chrome or Firefox for best compatibility
Strictly Prohibited:
  • • Using notes, books, or reference materials
  • • Having other people in the room
  • • Using mobile phone or other devices
  • • Taking screenshots or recordings
  • • Looking away from camera for extended periods
Technical Issues:
  • • Camera not working: Check browser permissions, do not restart browser
  • • Audio issues: Test microphone before starting, check system volume
  • • Browser crashes: Rejoin immediately, violations may be excused
Reminders:
  • • Dress professionally as this is a formal interview
  • • Speak clearly and confidently - you're being evaluated
  • • Don't panic if you make mistakes - stay calm and continue
  • • After ending interview, wait for redirect - don't close browser
